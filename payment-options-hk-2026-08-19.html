Payment Options for a Hong Kong Quiz Website

Scope: a low-budget, mobile-first, multilingual quiz website that receives funds in Hong Kong.

Recommendation: use Airwallex instead of Stripe for the first launch.
It has a free entry plan, hosted checkout/payment links, global cards and local methods, and slightly lower published card fees. It also supports subscription management if that becomes useful later.

Comparison

OptionBest forPublished cost / limitation
AirwallexBest all-round Stripe replacement for this launch.Free Explore plan. Domestic cards: 3.30% + HK$2.35; international cards: 3.60% + HK$2.35. Hosted checkout, payment links, Apple Pay/Google Pay, and local methods are available.
PayPal BusinessOptional second button for buyers who strongly prefer PayPal.Domestic commercial payments: 3.90% + HK$2.35; international payments add a percentage fee. Better buyer familiarity, but not the cheapest primary checkout.
PayMe for BusinessHong Kong customers only.1.5% for website/API transactions. Useful later as an additional local method, not as a global primary processor. Website/app access may require onboarding or a partner integration.
PaddleGlobal digital-product tax handling.Merchant-of-record model can simplify VAT/GST/sales-tax administration. Confirm onboarding eligibility and commercial terms before choosing it; it is not automatically the lowest-fee option.

Simple launch setup

  1. Use Airwallex hosted checkout so payment details never touch your DigitalOcean server.
  2. Sell only one paid product first: a full-batch unlock at HK$99–199.
  3. Add PayPal as a secondary checkout choice only if buyers request it.
  4. Add PayMe only when Hong Kong becomes a meaningful customer market.
