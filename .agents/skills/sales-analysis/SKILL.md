# Sales Analysis

Use when a commercial decision depends on first-party performance data. Define the measurement window, source, population, metric formula, known gaps, and comparison baseline before drawing conclusions.

Analyse the funnel where data exists: impression -> click -> landing view -> quiz start -> paywall reach -> purchase -> completion -> support/refund. Identify the earliest material drop-off and distinguish observed breakage from a possible cause.

Report FACT, OBSERVATION, HYPOTHESIS, and RECOMMENDATION. Do not label a causal explanation as fact without a valid comparison or test. Each hypothesis needs the full record under AGENTS.md and a next test.

Recommend measurement, product, or marketing experiments only. Price changes, paid marketing, publishing, deployment, and destructive actions require explicit owner approval.
