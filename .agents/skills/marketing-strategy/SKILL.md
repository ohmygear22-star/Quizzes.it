# Marketing Strategy

Use when deciding how to position or market an approved quiz. Re-research current audience language, competitor positioning, relevant content patterns, search/social signals, and available first-party performance. Do not assume a prior winning hook is still best.

Offer several genuinely distinct angles. For each, state audience, tension, value proposition, channel fit, evidence, confidence, measurement plan, and risks. Never copy competitor creative, distinctive wording, or paid content.

Separate FACT, OBSERVATION, HYPOTHESIS, and RECOMMENDATION. Each hypothesis needs a measurable comparison and full record under AGENTS.md.

Any spend, paid campaign, budget change, or external launch requires MARKETING_REVIEW and explicit owner approval. This skill prepares proposals only.
