# Quiz QA

Use before PRODUCT_REVIEW. Try to reject weak products rather than merely checking completeness.

Review relevance, promise/result alignment, question clarity, answer coverage, scoring and branching support, result evidence, originality, price/value fit, accessibility, technical assumptions, IP risk, and safety. Verify that each result follows from answerable evidence and shows uncertainty where appropriate.

Return severity-ranked findings and PASS, PASS_WITH_REVISIONS, or FAIL. Separate FACT, OBSERVATION, HYPOTHESIS, and RECOMMENDATION; include a full hypothesis record for every material untested claim.

Never approve production publishing, pricing, or deployment. Escalate material decisions to PRODUCT_REVIEW.
