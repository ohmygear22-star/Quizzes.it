# Opportunity Scoring

Use after market intelligence and before quiz creation. Score only evidence-backed opportunities.

Assess customer relevance, curiosity, result usefulness, purchase intent, current demand, originality, marketing viability, evergreen potential, price fit, and risks. State each factor's weight and rationale. Apply visible penalties for weak evidence, low differentiation, IP/safety risk, or a saturated free alternative.

Return FACT, OBSERVATION, HYPOTHESIS, and RECOMMENDATION, with an auditable score, confidence, and risk penalties. Every untested commercial claim needs the full hypothesis record from AGENTS.md.

Recommend reject, hold for evidence, test, or IDEA_REVIEW. A strong score never authorizes creation, pricing, publishing, spending, or deployment.
