# Market Intelligence

Use when a decision depends on current demand, audience behaviour, competition, pricing signals, or trend momentum. Define the target customer and decision first; do not draft quiz questions or launch a campaign.

Retrieve attributable, recent evidence and compare appropriate time windows. Distinguish attention, engagement, and purchase intent. A viral topic is not automatically a viable paid product.

Report FACT, OBSERVATION, HYPOTHESIS, and RECOMMENDATION. Include sources, observation dates, retrieval window, confidence, limitations, and the full hypothesis record required by AGENTS.md.

Study competitor patterns only: target audience, positioning, offer, price presentation, title structure, and funnel shape. Never reproduce questions, results, distinctive wording, creative, or paid content.

If current evidence is unavailable or insufficient, state that clearly and do not make a trend claim.
